Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how FlyHigh Institute collects, uses, stores and protects your personal data when you explore aviation training opportunities on our website.

1. What Information We Collect

FlyHigh Institute collects only the information needed to support your aviation training journey, course enquiries, and career counseling requests.

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number and city.
  • Course preferences, application notes, and enquiry details submitted via the website.
  • Device and browser data for website performance, page analytics and security.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected data to provide relevant training information, respond to admissions enquiries, and improve our digital experience.

  • To contact you regarding course updates, admissions, fees, and aviation career services.
  • To personalize information about Cabin Crew training, CPL, Ground Staff and Airport Management programs.
  • To optimize the website, analyze candidate preferences, and keep our platform secure.

3. Cookies, Tracking and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and tracking tools to measure traffic, track user engagement, and provide a consistent browsing experience.

  • Technical cookies to keep the website stable and responsive.
  • Analytics cookies to understand how visitors discover and use our pages.
  • No sensitive personal data is stored in cookies on this site.

4. Data Security

Protecting your personal data is our priority. We maintain reasonable security controls to prevent unauthorized access and data misuse.

  • Administrative controls for staff access to candidate enquiries and records.
  • Physical and electronic safeguards consistent with best practices in India.
  • Periodic reviews of our data handling processes to keep information safe.

5. Third Party Providers

FlyHigh Institute may share limited data with trusted service providers when required to process enquiries or manage site infrastructure.

  • Third parties helping with email delivery, analytics and website hosting.
  • Admission support partners assisting with application follow-up and course communication.
  • Links to external sites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction or deletion of your personal data, subject to applicable laws in India.

  • Request details of information we hold about you.
  • Ask to correct or update any inaccurate details on record.
  • Opt out of marketing communication while continuing to receive operational updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or requests related to this privacy policy, please contact our admissions team.
